
**Misfiring jobs: `target_scanner` and `perform_check`**

The `target_scanner` and `perform_check` jobs were consistently missing their scheduled fire time by about 1.2 seconds, both getting flagged as misfires. This didn't reproduce on y540-server, despite that machine running more targets (10, versus 3 on the personal machine), which pointed away from load-from-target-count as the cause.

Stopping the Prometheus container on the personal machine changed the picture: `perform_check` stopped misfiring entirely, but `target_scanner` still occasionally missed. I suspect this points to resource contention rather than a logic bug, likely tied to the mix of synchronous and asynchronous code in the project competing for the same thread pool or event loop.

**Fix:** increased `misfire_grace_time` to 2 seconds for both job classes. That gave enough tolerance to absorb the delay without the jobs being skipped.
